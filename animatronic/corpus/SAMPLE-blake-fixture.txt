A TEST FIXTURE, NOT THE CORPUS.

A few well-known Blake excerpts, here only so the lexicon pipeline can be
tested end to end. A lexicon built from this file is far too small to run the
piece — it would give the sculpture a vocabulary of a few hundred words.

Replace this directory with Blake's complete works before building the real
lexicon. Project Gutenberg has Songs of Innocence and of Experience, The
Marriage of Heaven and Hell, the prophetic books, and the collected poems;
download the plain-text editions into this directory and run:

    python -m anima.lexicon build corpus/*.txt -o lexicon/blake.json

---

Tyger Tyger, burning bright,
In the forests of the night;
What immortal hand or eye,
Could frame thy fearful symmetry?

In what distant deeps or skies
Burnt the fire of thine eyes?
On what wings dare he aspire?
What the hand, dare seize the fire?

I wander thro' each charter'd street,
Near where the charter'd Thames does flow.
And mark in every face I meet
Marks of weakness, marks of woe.

To see a World in a Grain of Sand
And a Heaven in a Wild Flower
Hold Infinity in the palm of your hand
And Eternity in an hour

O Rose thou art sick.
The invisible worm,
That flies in the night
In the howling storm

Little Lamb who made thee
Dost thou know who made thee
Gave thee life and bid thee feed
By the stream and o'er the mead
